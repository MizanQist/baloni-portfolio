Your brief

A private office, not a brochure.

Dear Mr Baloni,

This office is yours alone. Nothing in it is on the open market in the form you see it here, and nothing in it was assembled for anyone else. The first wing is open: eight residential addresses across Abuja and Victoria Island, each with its price, its drawings and our honest view.

Mark whatever interests you with the star. Your requests gather on your desk, and one message from there puts me to work: a viewing at the hour you choose, the full drawings or a valuation. I am on retainer to you, not to any developer or dealer, so the view you read on every page is the one I would give you across a table.

The office stays open. As new addresses reach us, they are added here first.
